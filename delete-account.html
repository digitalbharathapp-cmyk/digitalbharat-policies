🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can request deletion of your Digital Bharat account and all associated data at any time through the app.

How to Delete Your Account

1 Open the Digital Bharat App

Launch the Digital Bharat app on your Android device and make sure you are logged in.

2 Go to Settings

Tap on the Settings icon (⚙️) in the bottom navigation bar or menu.

3 Tap "Delete My Account"

Scroll down to the Account section and tap "Delete My Account".

4 Confirm Deletion

Read the warning and tap "Yes, Delete My Account" to confirm. Your account will be scheduled for deletion.

⚠️ 30-Day Grace Period

After you request account deletion, there is a 30-day grace period during which your account is deactivated but not permanently deleted. If you log back in during this period, your account will be restored. After 30 days, all your data will be permanently and irreversibly deleted.

What Data Gets Deleted

Data Type Action Timeframe
Profile Information (Name, Email, Photo) Permanently Deleted 30 days
Posts & Comments Permanently Deleted 30 days
Chat Messages Permanently Deleted 30 days
Shop Listings Permanently Deleted 30 days
Location Data Permanently Deleted 30 days
Uploaded Photos & Media Permanently Deleted 30 days
Notification Preferences Permanently Deleted 30 days
Blocked Users List Permanently Deleted 30 days

Request Data Deletion Without Deleting Account

If you want specific data removed (e.g., a particular post or photo) without deleting your entire account, you can:

  • Delete individual posts from your profile
  • Remove photos from your shop listings
  • Clear your chat messages
  • Contact us at the email below for any specific data removal requests
